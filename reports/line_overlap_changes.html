Graded by line overlap · changes

363 CNN proposals against 85 reviewer locations on 150 tiles, scored against a real 2023→2025 change in pedestrian infrastructure.

Overlap, not distance. A proposal is matched by how much of its length runs along a reviewer's line, not by whether some point of it comes within a radius. Two lines that run together and then fork score near 0.5 on one of the two coverages — which is the answer, and the one a distance test cannot give. Buffer caps are flat, so a proposal that overshoots its target is charged for the overshoot.
Identity is (tile, _id). The CNN's addition ids restart per city — add_edge_000001 exists in all five — so _id alone collides on 2,551 of 7,404 proposals and would attach one city's geometry to another's tile. Every proposal carries its own tile tag, and the pair is unique corpus-wide, so nothing here is matched by proximity of identity. Deletions additionally carry a real OSM id and Ricky's own IoU, shown in the last column for comparison.
